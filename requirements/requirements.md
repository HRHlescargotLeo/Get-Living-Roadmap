# Get Living — prototype requirements (V1)

Source: ClerksWell phase 1 review of getliving.com and five competitors (28 Sep 2026).
Idea numbers (#nn) refer to the Opportunities page. These are ClerksWell proposals, not
client requirements; Get Living has not yet reviewed them.

## Global
- R01 Header gives access to Locations, Homes, Renting with us, The Edit and a saved-homes count; Make an enquiry is always one click away.
- R02 Every template works at 320px, 768px and 1024px+; filters, galleries and booking are usable on mobile.
- R03 All interactive components are keyboard operable and dismissible with Escape (WCAG 2.2 AA target) (#50).
- R04 Saved homes persist across pages in the session and show a count in the header (#4).

## Prototype 1 — Find a home (ideas #1–#10)
- R10 Filters for location/neighbourhood, bedrooms, max rent and move-in date sit in a sticky bar, not a collapsed accordion (#1).
- R11 A "More filters" drawer adds furnished, pets, balcony, parking, accessible home and home type (rental / co-living) (#6, #10).
- R12 Results can be shown as a list or on a map, grouped by neighbourhood and building (#2).
- R13 Each card shows rent, bedrooms, size (sq ft and m²), floor, bathrooms, furnished status and available-from date (#3).
- R14 Renters can save homes and compare up to three side by side (#4).
- R15 Renters can save a search and get alerts; an empty result offers a waitlist (#5).
- R16 Results load 12 at a time with "Load more"; sort by price and availability (#7).
- R17 Offers are shown as a consistent badge where they apply (#9).

## Prototype 2 — Property page (ideas #11–#18, #29)
- R20 A key facts panel sits beside the gallery: rent, size, floor, bathrooms, EPC, council tax band, furnished, available from, pets (#11).
- R21 A monthly cost estimate shows rent, what's included at £0, and estimated bills, adjustable for occupants and extras (#12).
- R22 A move-in cost breakdown shows reservation payment, deposit or deposit alternative and first month's rent, with when each is due (#13).
- R23 Description follows a short house style with amenities as a list, not capitals (#14).
- R24 Book a viewing and Enquire are the primary actions; Save and Share are secondary (#15).
- R25 Getting around: walk times and a commute checker on every home (#17).
- R26 Similar homes and a link to all homes in the building (#18).
- R27 Deposit protection and client money protection shown beside the cost breakdown (#29).

## Prototype 3 — Viewings and enquiries (ideas #15, #19, #20, #22)
- R30 Book a viewing in three steps: type, slot, details — with the home already filled in (#15).
- R31 Viewing types: in person, video call with the team, live group video tour (#20).
- R32 Confirmation with add-to-calendar and what happens next.
- R33 A short general enquiry: name, email, phone, neighbourhood, move-in date; everything else optional or later (#19).
- R34 Overseas renters are signposted to video viewings and a guide (#22).

## Prototype 4 — Renting with us (ideas #23–#27)
- R40 Tenancy copy reflects the Renters' Rights Act (periodic tenancies from 1 May 2026) (#23).
- R41 A plain-English explainer of what the Act means for a Get Living resident (#24).
- R42 The renting process shown as its real sequence of steps.
- R43 A comparison of Get Living with renting from a typical private landlord (#26).
- R44 Verified review score and resident survey figures (#25, #27).

## Prototype 5 — Neighbourhood template (ideas #30–#35)
- R50 One template for all six neighbourhoods with a sticky in-page nav (#30).
- R51 "From £" prices and live availability by home size, linking to filtered search (#31).
- R52 Interactive map with layers for transport, green space, food and drink, fitness, schools and health (#32).
- R53 Walk times and commute checker (#33).
- R54 Neighbourhood FAQs (#34).
- R55 Public what's-on events, marked open or residents only (#35).
- R56 Verified reviews for the neighbourhood (#25).
