What we're proposing

One template for all six neighbourhood pages, shown here with Sherlock Quarter. Each page carries live availability and prices by home size, a map with layers for transport, green space and local amenities, journey times, reviews, events and questions specific to that neighbourhood.

Why

The neighbourhood pages vary a good deal. East Village has a commute calculator and detailed transport times; others have much less. None of the pages we looked at show what homes cost or how many are available, so a renter who likes the sound of a place has to leave the page and start a search to find out. A shared template keeps the six pages consistent, and neighbourhood-level questions and answers give search engines, and the AI tools people increasingly search with, something specific to quote.

Availability and prices come from the listings (sample data here), so they stay current without anyone editing the page.
