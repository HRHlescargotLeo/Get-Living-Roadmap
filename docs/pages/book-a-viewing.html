Prototype 3 of 5 · Book a viewing

What we're proposing

Let renters book a viewing themselves: choose in person, a video call or a live group tour, pick a slot, and leave a name and contact details. The confirmation arrives straight away. Alongside it sits a shorter enquiry form for people who aren't ready to book.

Why

At the moment, arranging a viewing starts with a request rather than a booking, and the enquiry form asks around 13 required questions on one screen, including employment status and how the renter heard about Get Living, before anything is arranged. Each extra field loses a few people, and the ones lost tend to be those a viewing would most likely have won over. The form also asks whether the renter lives in the UK, which suggests enough overseas interest to justify video viewings. The extra detail is still useful to the leasing team. It just doesn't need collecting before the first conversation.

The home is carried over from the property page. Slots are sample data; live availability would come from the leasing team's calendar.
