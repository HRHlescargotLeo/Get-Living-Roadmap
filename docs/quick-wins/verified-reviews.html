Quick win 12 of 28 · Proposition and trust

What we're proposing

An independent review score and recent verified reviews from HomeViews, alongside the testimonials Get Living has chosen.

Why

Get Living has 1,357 verified reviews on HomeViews with an average of 4.42, but the site shows three testimonials in a carousel. Renters tend to trust an independent score more than a quote the company chose. This is mostly a matter of placing the score and a few recent reviews on the right pages.
