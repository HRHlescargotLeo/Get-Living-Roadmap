Quick win 23 of 28 · Residents

What we're proposing

Move the referral offer from a banner on every page to a personal share link for residents.

Why

The 'Refer a Friend' banner sits above every page, so most of the people who see it are prospective renters it doesn't apply to. Residents, who can use it, have to go looking. A personal link in the resident area or app, ready to copy or send, puts the £100 offer in front of the right people.
