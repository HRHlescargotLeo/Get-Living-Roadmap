Quick win 26 of 28 · Look and feel

What we're proposing

Let the photography run at full strength, with the headline on a solid panel instead of a white wash over the photo.

Why

To keep the black headline readable, the homepage and neighbourhood heroes lay a heavy white wash over the photo, which flattens some of Get Living's best photography. Setting the headline on a solid sage panel beside the image keeps it readable and lets the photo do its job. It is a template and CSS change.
