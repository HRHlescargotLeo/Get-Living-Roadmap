Quick win 3 of 28 · Finding a home

What we're proposing

One consistent offer badge on the photo, instead of offers written into the description in capitals.

Why

Incentives are currently written into the listing text ("MOVE IN NOW!", "MOVE IN OCTOBER!"), which makes them easy to miss and impossible to filter. A badge driven from a field in the CMS can be switched on and off by the leasing team, and the same field can power an "Offers" filter later.
