Quick win 2 of 28 · Finding a home

What we're proposing

Twelve homes at a time with a "Load more" button, instead of six per page with numbered pages.

Why

Search results currently arrive six at a time, with numbered pages underneath. Every page turn is one more step between the renter and the home they are looking for. Showing twelve and loading more in place keeps people browsing, and it is a setting and a small piece of front-end work rather than a new feature.
