Quick wins

28 smaller changes to getliving.com, each shown on its own against the site as it is today. Any of them can be made independently of the five major improvements, and of each other.

Open one, then use Today and With this change to compare. Switch Notes on to see why we suggest it.

Finding a home

  • Quick win 1

    Show the facts renters compare on every card

    Size, floor, bathrooms and furnished status on every search result, in place of a paragraph of capitals.

    Also part of prototype 1, Find a home.
  • Quick win 2

    Show more results per page

    Twelve homes at a time with a "Load more" button, instead of six per page with numbered pages.

    Also part of prototype 1, Find a home.
  • Quick win 3

    Show offers on the card

    One consistent offer badge on the photo, instead of offers written into the description in capitals.

  • Quick win 4

    Make co-living easy to find

    Co-living gets its own entry in the Homes menu and a short landing page, rather than living only inside the search filter.

The property page

  • Quick win 5

    A key facts panel at the top of every home

    Rent, size, floor, EPC, council tax band, furnished, availability and pets in one panel beside the photos.

    Also part of prototype 2, Property page.
  • Quick win 6

    Break down the cost of moving in

    A short table of what is paid, and when, before moving in.

    Also part of prototype 2, Property page.
  • Quick win 7

    Rewrite listing copy to a house style

    Short, plain descriptions with amenities as a list, instead of capitals for emphasis.

  • Quick win 8

    Put journey times on the home, not just the neighbourhood

    Walk times to stations and landmarks, and a commute checker, on every property page.

    Also part of prototype 2, Property page.
  • Quick win 9

    Show similar homes, and the rest of the building

    The block at the foot of each home shows genuinely similar homes and links to everything else in the same building.

    Also part of prototype 2, Property page.

Proposition and trust

  • Quick win 10

    Update tenancy copy for the Renters' Rights Act

    Replace the three-year tenancy wording with copy that reflects rolling tenancies.

    Also part of prototype 4, Renting with us.
  • Quick win 11

    Explain the Renters' Rights Act in plain English

    A short explainer of what the new rules mean for a Get Living resident, linked from search and property pages.

    Also part of prototype 4, Renting with us.
  • Quick win 12

    Show verified reviews

    An independent review score and recent verified reviews from HomeViews, alongside the testimonials Get Living has chosen.

    Also part of prototypes 4 and 5.
  • Quick win 13

    Compare renting with Get Living and a typical private let

    A simple comparison table alongside the existing "10 reasons to rent" list.

    Also part of prototype 4, Renting with us.
  • Quick win 14

    Back up the service claims with resident survey figures

    Two or three headline figures from Get Living's own resident survey.

  • Quick win 15

    Show accreditations where money changes hands

    Deposit and client money protection logos next to the deposit information, not only in the footer.

    Also part of prototype 2, Property page.

Neighbourhoods

  • Quick win 16

    "From" prices by home size on neighbourhood pages

    Each home size on a neighbourhood page shows its lowest live rent and how many are available.

    Also part of prototype 5, Neighbourhoods.
  • Quick win 17

    The commute calculator on every neighbourhood

    The commute checker East Village already has, on every neighbourhood page.

    Also part of prototype 5, Neighbourhoods.
  • Quick win 18

    Questions and answers for each neighbourhood

    Eight to ten FAQs per neighbourhood, written from what is already in the listings.

    Also part of prototype 5, Neighbourhoods.

Content and search visibility

  • Quick win 19

    A newsletter and "new homes first" sign-up

    A light email sign-up on The Edit, neighbourhood pages and empty search results.

  • Quick win 20

    Structured data for homes, reviews and FAQs

    Behind-the-scenes markup that makes pages eligible for richer results in search engines and AI tools.

  • Quick win 21

    A page for pet owners

    One page on renting with a pet: the policy in plain English, dog-friendly spaces and pet events.

  • Quick win 22

    A page on energy and sustainability

    One page on energy performance, what it saves residents, EV charging and bike storage.

Residents

  • Quick win 23

    Make referrals easy to share

    Move the referral offer from a banner on every page to a personal share link for residents.

  • Quick win 24

    Show prospective renters what residents get

    A short "What residents get" section with partner offers and events, on the pages prospective renters see.

Look and feel

  • Quick win 25

    Search in the homepage hero

    A compact search under the homepage headline, so renters can start looking straight away.

  • Quick win 26

    Sharper hero imagery

    Let the photography run at full strength, with the headline on a solid panel instead of a white wash over the photo.

  • Quick win 27

    A more useful mobile menu

    Search and the six neighbourhoods at the top level of the mobile menu.

  • Quick win 28

    Motion with restraint

    A few deliberate moments of movement: cards that rise into place and lift when you point at them.
