Quick win 6 of 28 · The property page

What we're proposing

A short table of what is paid, and when, before moving in.

Why

Deposit options, the reservation payment and when the first month's rent is due are all explained somewhere, across the listing and the FAQs. A renter working out whether they can afford to move has to assemble the figures themselves. A small table on each home, calculated from the rent, answers it in one place.
