Five ideas for getliving.com

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the Get Living website. Each one works as it would on the live site, designed to sit with the current Get Living brand.

Version 2 28 September 2026 Designed prototypes Sample data

The prototypes

  1. Find a home
    1

    A better way to find a home

    Filters that stay in view, a map, the facts renters compare on every card, saved homes, side-by-side comparison and alerts when a match comes up.

    Try: filter to Birmingham, save two homes, then tick Compare on both.

    Open prototype
  2. Property page
    2

    A property page that shows the real cost

    Key facts beside the photos, an estimate of monthly bills, a clear breakdown of what's due before moving in, journey times and similar homes.

    Try: change the number of people living there, then switch the deposit option.

    Open prototype
  3. Book a viewing
    3

    Book a viewing in under a minute

    Choose in person, video call or a live group tour, pick a slot and confirm. Alongside it, a shorter enquiry form with five required fields instead of around 13.

    Try: book a live group tour and see which days are offered.

    Open prototype
  4. Renting with us
    4

    Renting with us, updated for the new rules

    A plain-English guide to the Renters' Rights Act, how renting with Get Living works step by step, a comparison with a typical private let, and verified reviews.

    Try: open the questions under "The Renters' Rights Act and what it means for you".

    Open prototype
  5. Neighbourhood
    5

    One template for every neighbourhood

    Shown with Sherlock Quarter: live availability and prices by home size, a map with layers, journey times, reviews, events and FAQs.

    Try: switch map layers on and off, then pick a home size.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: filters, map pins, saving, comparing, the cost estimate, booking steps and forms. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Get Living (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Listings are sample data. Neighbourhood and building names and rent levels follow the live site; sizes, floors, dates and offers are illustrative.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.
