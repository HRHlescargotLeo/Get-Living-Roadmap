Quick win 7 of 28 · The property page

What we're proposing

Short, plain descriptions with amenities as a list, instead of capitals for emphasis.

Why

Many listings use capitals to pick out features ("2 BEDROOM FULLY FURNISHED", "GYM"), and the same paragraph repeats across homes in a building. It reads as shouting and makes every home sound alike. A one-page copy guide for the leasing team and a one-off clean-up of live listings is mostly writing, not development.
