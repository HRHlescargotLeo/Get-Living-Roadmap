Search and the six neighbourhoods at the top level of the mobile menu.

Why

On a phone, the menu shows four headings and three shortcuts, and the neighbourhoods are two taps away under 'Locations'. Someone opening the menu on a phone is most likely looking for a home in a particular place, so putting search and the neighbourhood list at the top level shortens the most common journey.
