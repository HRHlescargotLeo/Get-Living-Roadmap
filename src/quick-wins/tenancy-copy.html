What we're proposing

Replace the three-year tenancy wording with copy that reflects rolling tenancies.

Why

'Living with us' still says tenancies are 'offered at three-years standard' with a break clause. New tenancies in England have been periodic since 1 May 2026, so the line is out of date whatever else the site does. This is a copy change, to be checked by Get Living's legal team.
