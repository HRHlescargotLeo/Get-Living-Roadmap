Quick win 4 of 28 · Finding a home

What we're proposing

Co-living gets its own entry in the Homes menu and a short landing page, rather than living only inside the search filter.

Why

Get Living offers co-living, but the only place we found it is the "I would like a" option at the top of the search filters. Anyone who might want it has to know it exists first. A menu entry and a short page explaining how it differs from renting a whole home costs very little and makes a product the business already runs visible.
