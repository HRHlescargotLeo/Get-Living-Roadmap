What we're proposing

Size, floor, bathrooms and furnished status on every search result, in place of a paragraph of capitals.

Why

People compare homes on a handful of facts: how big, which floor, how many bathrooms, furnished or not. Today's cards lead with a paragraph of description that leaves most of those out, so the renter has to open each home to find them. Putting four facts on the card is a template change, and the data almost certainly exists already in the letting system.
