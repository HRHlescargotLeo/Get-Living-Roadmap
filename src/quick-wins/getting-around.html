Quick win 8 of 28 · The property page

What we're proposing

Walk times to stations and landmarks, and a commute checker, on every property page.

Why

Each listing has a 'Your location' paragraph, and East Village's neighbourhood page has a commute calculator, but the property pages have neither a map nor a way to check a journey. Commute is one of the first things a renter checks. The calculator already exists on the site; reusing it on property pages is a small change.
