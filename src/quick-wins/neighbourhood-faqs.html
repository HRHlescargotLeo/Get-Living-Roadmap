What we're proposing

Why

Questions specific to a neighbourhood – parking, heating, council tax band, pets – are answered today in individual listings or in the general FAQs. Gathering them on the neighbourhood page helps renters, and marked up as FAQs they give search engines and AI search tools something specific to quote.
