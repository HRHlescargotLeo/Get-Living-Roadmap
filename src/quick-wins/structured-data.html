What we're proposing

Behind-the-scenes markup that makes pages eligible for richer results in search engines and AI tools.

Why

Get Living's enquiry form lists 'AI search, e.g. ChatGPT, Gemini' as a source, so some renters are already finding it that way. Standard schema.org markup for listings, FAQs and reviews tells search engines and AI tools exactly what each page contains. Whether a richer result appears is up to the search engine; the markup makes the page eligible. Nothing changes visually on the site itself.
