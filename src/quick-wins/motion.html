What we're proposing

A few deliberate moments of movement: cards that rise into place and lift when you point at them.

Why

Apart from the carousels, the site is largely still. A small amount of considered motion – content easing into place, cards lifting on hover – makes it feel more current without getting in the way, and it respects visitors who have asked their device to reduce motion.
