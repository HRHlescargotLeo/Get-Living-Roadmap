What we're proposing

Move the facts that currently sit at the bottom of each listing, under 'The fine print', up beside the photos, and add two things the page doesn't yet have: an estimate of what it costs to live there each month, and a breakdown of what has to be paid before moving in. Journey times and similar homes follow further down.

Why

Everything a renter needs to work out the real cost of a home is already on the page – the deposit options, how hot water is billed, the council tax band, parking charges – but it is written as paragraphs of small print at the end. On HomeViews, residents rate Get Living lowest on value, at 4.0 out of 5 against 4.42 overall. That may say as much about how costs are explained as about the costs themselves. A page that answers the money question up front should also cut the number of enquiries that exist only to ask it.

Open any home from Find a home to load it here; by default it shows a two-bedroom at Printers Place. Figures marked as estimates are illustrative.
