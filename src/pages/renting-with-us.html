The Renters' Rights Act and what it means for you

New rules for renting in England started on 1 May 2026. We welcome them: most of what they ask of landlords is how we already work. Here's what they mean if you rent with us.

Your tenancy rolls on month by month, with no fixed end date. You can stay as long as you like, as long as you keep to the terms of your tenancy.

Give us two months' notice in writing, at any time. There's no break clause to wait for.

We can review rent once a year. We'll give you at least two months' written notice of any change, and you have the right to challenge it at a tribunal if you think it's above the market rate.

Yes. Pets have always been welcome at Get Living, at no extra cost. You'll sign our pet policy and register your pet with the team.

A reservation payment of up to one week's rent, a deposit (or a deposit alternative), and your first month's rent once you've signed. Every property page shows the exact amounts.

Yes. Existing tenancies moved onto the new rolling terms on 1 May 2026. Your resident team can answer any questions about your own tenancy.

How renting with us works

  1. Find a home

    Search by neighbourhood, size, budget and move-in date, and save the ones you like.

  2. View it

    In person, on a video call or on a live group tour. Book a slot online.

  3. Reserve

    Pay a reservation of up to one week's rent to take the home off the market.

  4. Referencing

    Quick online checks with our referencing partner. No fees.

  5. Move in

    Sign online, pay your deposit and first month, and collect your keys from the team.
