Prototype 1 of 5 · Find a home

What we're proposing

A search page built around the questions renters ask of a listing: where it is, how big it is, when it's free and what it costs. The filters sit in a bar that stays on screen as you scroll, rather than inside a closed panel, and results can be shown as a list or on a map. Homes can be saved and compared side by side, and a search can be saved so that new matches arrive by email.

Why

The current Find a home page keeps its filters behind a single 'Filter' button and shows six homes at a time, so on the day we looked, the 79 homes available ran to nine pages. The cards lead with a paragraph of description but leave out floor area, floor and number of bathrooms, which are the details people use to weigh one home against another. Most renters will look at several homes, often in more than one building, before booking anything, and the site currently gives them no way to keep a shortlist. When nothing matches, the only route offered is a general enquiry.

Everything works with sample listings: filters, list and map, save, compare, alerts and load more.
